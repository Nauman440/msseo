What does the free SEO audit include?

Do I need to know SEO to use it?

No. Every issue is explained in plain language, and the tool prepares the fix for you. You only decide whether to approve it.

No. You see the old and new version of every change before it goes live, and you can undo any change with one click. Risky changes such as robots.txt, redirects and new pages always wait for your approval.

WordPress and Shopify connect directly. Other website builders can use a simple code snippet, and the free audit works on any public website.

An SEO tool gives you a list of problems and leaves the work to you. An agency does the work, usually for a high monthly retainer. We prepare and apply the fixes like an agency, at a software price, and you keep control of every change.

Yes. We track how AI assistants answer the questions your customers ask, then improve the content, schema and trust signals they rely on. Nobody can guarantee a mention, so we report the trend honestly.
